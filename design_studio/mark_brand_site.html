About the Studio

A brand is not what you say it is. It is what they feel when they encounter you.

Mark was founded on a single principle: that the most valuable thing a business can own is a strong identity. Not a logo. Not a colour palette. An identity — a coherent, resonant, enduring presence that compounds in value with every impression it makes. We work with clients who are ready to invest in that kind of permanence. Our process is rigorous, our standards are absolute, and our output is designed to outlast every trend that comes and goes.
