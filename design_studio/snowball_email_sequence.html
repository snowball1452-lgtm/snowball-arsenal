Objection Handling

"We already have a website."
"I know — I looked at it before reaching out. My question is whether it currently converts at the level your work deserves. Most luxury studios have a site that describes their work rather than sells it. That's the gap I close."
"We have a developer / agency."
"Most developers build functional sites. I build sites that are specifically calibrated to the luxury design market — the aesthetic, the pacing, the copy, the conversion flow. It's a niche expertise that generalist developers don't have."
"What's the price?"
"Commissions start at $3,500 for a complete site and run to $8,500 for full brand identity plus site plus content. The right answer depends on what you need — that's what the 15-minute call is for. There's no commitment in the call."
"We don't have budget right now."
"Understood. When does your budget cycle reset? I keep a waitlist for Q-ahead slots — I can put your name down so when budget clears you're not waiting months. No commitment required."
"Can you show me more examples?"
"Yes — I'll send you our full portfolio deck. Each example includes before/after and the business outcome. What vertical are you most interested in? I'll lead with the most relevant."
"How long does it take?"
"7 business days from brief sign-off to live site. That includes design, build, copywriting, and deployment. We move fast because our process is tightly defined — no endless revision cycles."

The 3-Step Close (On a Call)

STEP 1 — DIAGNOSE (5 minutes) "Tell me about your current site. What do you like about it, what frustrates you, and what do you wish it did better?" Listen for: lost leads, mismatch between site and quality of work, embarrassment when sending the URL. STEP 2 — PRESENT (5 minutes) "Let me show you what we built for [similar client in their vertical]." Screen share your best demo site. "This client had the same challenge — their work was exceptional, their site wasn't. We fixed that in 7 days." STEP 3 — CLOSE (3 minutes) "Based on what you've told me, the [Precision / Elegance / Technical] archetype would be the right fit for you. A complete commission is [price]. I have one slot open [this month / next month]. Want to move forward?" If yes: send contract immediately — same day. If maybe: "What would need to be true for you to say yes?" If no: "Understood. Can I ask what the main reason is?" — then handle the objection above.
