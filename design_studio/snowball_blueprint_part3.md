    ## 10. SALES PLAYBOOK — CLOSING $5K SITES
    
    ### The 3-Step Close
    
    **Step 1: The Concept Hook (Day 0)**
    Send them a 60-second Loom video showing their current site alongside one of your Snowball demos.
    Don't pitch. Just say: "I made this in 20 minutes. Wanted to show you what's possible."
    
    **Step 2: The Discovery Call (Day 2-3)**
    30-minute call. Ask:
    - "What kind of clients are you trying to attract?"
    - "What do your best clients say about you?"
    - "What's the ONE thing your site should make someone feel?"
    - "When do you want this done?"
    
    **Step 3: The Proposal (Day 3-4)**
    Send a 1-page proposal. Include:
    - 2 archetype options (Dark Precision vs Light Elegance)
    - Clear deliverables list
    - Timeline
    - Price (one number, no options — options kill deals)
    - "50% deposit to start, 50% on delivery"
    
    **Objection Handling:**
    
    | Objection | Response |
    |-----------|----------|
    | "It's too expensive" | "What does one new client worth to you? This site will close more than one." |
    | "I can do it myself" | "You could. But you're spending your time designing, not building websites." |
    | "I need to think about it" | "Totally fair. I have one slot in [month]. Want me to hold it?" |
    | "Can you do it cheaper?" | "I can't do this quality for less. But I can do a smaller scope at $2,500." |
