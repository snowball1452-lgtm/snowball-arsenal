3.1 Project Timeline. Snowball shall deliver the completed project within _______ business days of receiving (a) the signed Agreement, (b) the deposit payment, and (c) all required Client-supplied materials.

4. Intellectual Property & Ownership

CLAUSE 4.1 — 4.4

4.1 Transfer of Ownership. Upon receipt of full payment, Snowball assigns to Client full ownership of the final Deliverables, including all design files and source code created specifically for this project.

4.2 Snowball Retained Rights. Snowball retains the right to: (a) display the work in its portfolio; (b) use the work in marketing materials; (c) describe the nature of services provided. Client may request portfolio exclusion in writing — Snowball will honor reasonable requests for sensitive or confidential projects.

4.3 Pre-Existing Materials. Snowball retains ownership of all pre-existing design systems, templates, frameworks, tools, and processes used to complete the project. Client receives a perpetual, royalty-free license to use these as incorporated into the Deliverables.

4.4 Third-Party Assets. Client is responsible for obtaining and maintaining licenses for any third-party assets (fonts, stock photography, plugins) incorporated into the project. Snowball shall disclose all third-party assets used.

5. Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

CLAUSE 5.1 — 5.5

5.1 Definition of Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party in connection with this Agreement, including but not limited to: business plans, financial data, client lists, product roadmaps, pricing, and proprietary processes.

5.2 Obligations. Each party agrees to: (a) hold Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose it to third parties without prior written consent; (c) use it only for purposes related to this Agreement; (d) apply at minimum the same protections used for its own confidential information.

5.3 Exclusions. Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party; (b) was rightfully known prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or court order.

5.4 Duration. Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for a period of 3 years from the date of disclosure.

5.5 Return of Materials. Upon request or termination, each party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information of the other party and certify such destruction in writing.

6. Warranties & Limitation of Liability

CLAUSE 6.1 — 6.3

6.1 Snowball Warranties. Snowball warrants that: (a) the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care; (b) the Deliverables will not knowingly infringe third-party intellectual property rights; (c) Snowball has authority to enter into this Agreement.

6.2 Client Warranties. Client warrants that: (a) all materials provided are owned by Client or Client has obtained necessary rights; (b) use of Client-provided materials will not infringe third-party rights; (c) Client has authority to enter into this Agreement.

6.3 Limitation of Liability. In no event shall either party be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages. Snowball's total liability for any claim arising from this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client in the 3 months preceding the claim.

7. Termination

CLAUSE 7.1 — 7.3

7.1 Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement with 7 days written notice. In the event of termination, Client shall pay for all work completed to date, calculated on a pro-rata basis against the total project fee.

7.2 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 7 days of written notice.

7.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Snowball shall deliver all work completed to date. Ownership of partially-completed work shall not transfer until all outstanding fees are paid in full.
