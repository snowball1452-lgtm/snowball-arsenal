    ## 02. THE SNOWBALL DESIGN SYSTEM — CURATED STACK
    
    ### Typography System (3 Tiers)
    
    **Tier 1 — Prestige Display (Headlines, Hero Text)**
    - Primary: PP Neue Montreal or Neue Haas Grotesk Display
    - Alternative (free): Inter Display or DM Sans at heavy weight
    - Characteristics: Geometric, tight tracking, authority without arrogance
    - Usage: H1, H2, hero text, section titles
    
    **Tier 2 — Editorial Body (Content, Descriptions)**
    - Primary: Freight Display Pro (serif for contrast) or Editorial New
    - Alternative (free): Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond
    - Characteristics: Elegant, readable, signals craft
    - Usage: Project descriptions, about text, long-form content
    
    **Tier 3 — Technical Caption (Labels, Nav, Meta)**
    - Primary: Space Mono or JetBrains Mono
    - Characteristics: Technical precision, monospaced = engineering credibility
    - Usage: Project codes, dates, sector labels, nav items
