    # PART THREE: LOCATIONS & STAR SYSTEMS
    
    ## The Sovereignty's Core Worlds
    
    ### VERATH PRIME — The Throne World
    **System:** Verath System, Galactic Core Adjacency Zone
    **Classification:** Ecumenopolis (Planet-City)
    **Population:** 14.7 billion registered; estimated 3–4 billion unregistered
    **Gravity:** 1.2 Standard
    **Atmosphere:** Maintained by continent-spanning atmospheric processors (Class IX)
    **Climate:** Controlled. Perpetual temperate across upper tiers; lower tiers run hot from industrial exhaust.
    
    Verath Prime is the undisputed center of Sovereignty power. The entire surface is built over — layer upon layer, going down eleven kilometers into the planetary crust. The upper three layers (the Pinnacle Districts) are home to A-rank and above. The middle layers (Transit Zones, Commercial Rings) house the working B and C-ranks. Everything below Layer 7 is called the Deep, and it operates by different rules.
    
    **Notable Districts:**
    - **The Spire of Arbitration** — The Sovereignty Council's seat of government. A single tower rising 4 km above the upper layer. Access requires B-rank minimum; top floors require S-rank escort.
    - **The Resonance Academies** — Seven competing institutions for ranked ability development. The top two (Aurelius and Thessan) only accept A-rank potential candidates.
    - **The Scrap Quarter Network** — The collective term for all Deep-level habitation zones. Ren Voss's home district, Sublevel 9-West, is one of ~800 such zones.
    - **The Black Concourse** — Layer 5. The largest unregulated market in the Sovereignty. Everything illegal is sold here. The Covenant looks the other way in exchange for 30% of transaction taxes.
    - **The Vault of First Ascension** — A sealed facility beneath Layer 10. The original location where the Architect first manifested the System 4,000 years ago. Off-limits. Guarded by six S-ranks permanently.
    
    **Power Infrastructure:**
    Verath Prime runs on a network of 847 Resonance Tap Stations — facilities that draw ambient Resonance energy from the Lattice and convert it to usable power. Each station is maintained by a team of B-rank Resonance Engineers. If more than 30 stations fail simultaneously, the atmospheric processors begin to degrade. The Succession has theorized about this weakness extensively.
    
    ---
    
    ### KETH'MARA — The War World
    **System:** Outer Massiva Cluster, Frontier Zone
    **Classification:** Fortified Combat World
    **Population:** 890 million (80% military or military-adjacent)
    **Gravity:** 1.6 Standard
    **Atmosphere:** Breathable but thin. High UV. Most inhabitants have adapted over generations.
    **Climate:** Harsh. Seasonal temperature swings of 80°C. Constant storm activity in the polar regions.
    
    The homeworld of the Keth species. The Sovereignty absorbed Keth'Mara 600 years ago — technically a treaty, practically a conquest. The Keth fought harder than any species the Sovereignty had ever encountered and lost only because the Architect refused to grant rank advancement to non-System-registered species until they joined. The Keth joined. They have not forgiven.
    
    **Notable Locations:**
    - **The Proving Grounds of Massiva** — A 900 km² combat training facility. The most brutal rank-advancement testing environment in the Sovereignty. 23% fatality rate for C-to-B advancement trials.
    - **The Bone Archives** — A Keth cultural institution housing the preserved remains of every Keth warrior who died unranked. Considered sacred. The Sovereignty attempted to demolish it once. The resulting uprising killed 40,000 Sovereignty soldiers.
    - **The Breach** — A 2 km wide scar in the planet's surface from a Succession assault 200 years ago. It has never been repaired. The Keth keep it open as a reminder.
    - **Clan-Hold Massiva-Prime** — The fortress of the largest Keth clan. Houses 180,000 Keth. Built into a mountain range. Has resisted six Sovereignty "compliance operations."
    
    ---
    
    ### ASHA'LEN — The Drift World
    **System:** The Expanse Fringe, coordinates classified by the Aethon
    **Classification:** Mobile Habitat Cluster (technically a world; practically a fleet)
    **Population:** 2.1 million Aethon
    **Gravity:** Variable (sections range from 0.3 to 0.9 Standard)
    **Atmosphere:** Each hab-module maintains its own environmental system
    
    Asha'Len is not a planet. It is a collection of 400+ interconnected habitat modules, asteroid-anchored stations, and converted vessels that the Aethon have linked together over 800 years of gradual accumulation. It drifts through a specific corridor of the Expanse, following a path that the Aethon claim is determined by Resonance currents visible only to them.
    
    **Notable Locations:**
    - **The Thread Halls** — The central linking structure. Where the collective Resonance of all Aethon aboard merges into a shared awareness. Non-Aethon visitors describe it as walking through music they can feel but not hear.
    - **The Remembrance Garden** — An artificial ecosystem sustaining 847 plant species from the Aethon homeworld (destroyed 1,200 years ago). The most protected location in the cluster.
    - **The Calculation Spire** — Where Aethon mathematicians map the Resonance Lattice. Their charts are more accurate than anything the Sovereignty produces, and they do not share them.
    - **The Silence Wing** — A section of Asha'Len that broadcasts no Resonance signature. Where Aethon go when they need to think without the collective. Outsiders are never admitted.
    
    ---
    
    ### CRUCIBLE STATION — The Arena World
    **System:** Neutral Zone, The Expanse Edge
    **Classification:** Orbital Combat-Commerce Platform
    **Population:** 3.4 million permanent; 800,000 transient
    **Gravity:** 0.9 Standard (station-generated)
    **Atmosphere:** Maintained. Breathable by all major species.
    
    Crucible Station is the largest independent facility in the known galaxy — not affiliated with the Sovereignty, the Dominion, or any faction. It operates under the Charter of Neutral Commerce, a document drafted by the six founding merchant houses 300 years ago and enforced by the station's own military arm (the Crucible Guard, ~40,000 strong, averaging B-rank).
    
    **Districts:**
    - **The Ring Markets** — 11 concentric commercial rings. Everything legal, semi-legal, and quietly illegal. The largest information brokerage in the galaxy (The Glass Archive) operates from Ring 4.
    - **The Pit** — The combat arena. The official version is ranked competition. The unofficial version runs three levels underground and has no rules. Orin Dusk owns Level 3.
    - **The Dock Sprawl** — 2,400 berths for vessels up to destroyer class. Capital ships anchor in external docking arms.
    - **The Quiet District** — Where factions meet when they don't want records. Surveilled by everyone. Recorded by no one (officially).
    - **The Underbelly** — The maintenance tunnels and sub-levels. Effectively a second city. Population estimated at 200,000 permanent unlicensed residents.
    
    ---
    
    ### THE DOMINION WORLDS
    **Primary World: VESK TERMINUS**
    **Classification:** Industrial Fortress World
    **Population:** 8.2 billion
    **Notable Feature:** The entire planet surface is covered in weapons manufacturing. The Dominion produces 40% of the Sovereignty's military hardware — and sells the other 60% to whoever can pay.
    
    The Dominion is not a rebel faction. It is a sanctioned competitor within the Sovereignty framework — a bloc of twelve worlds that achieved enough economic power to negotiate semi-autonomy 400 years ago. They pay Sovereignty tithes. They follow Sovereignty law. They simply also do whatever they want within their own borders.
    
    **Secondary Worlds:**
    - **Vesk Null** — A gas giant with 14 processing stations. Fuel production. 90% of faster-than-light capable vessels in the galaxy refuel from Vesk Null derivatives.
    - **The Gray Stations** — 40 orbital facilities scattered through Dominion space. Manufacturing, research, weapons testing. Three of them are off the Sovereignty's official maps.
    - **Kira's Fall** — A dead world. Was a Succession stronghold. The Dominion glassed it 80 years ago. No survivors. The Dominion does not discuss why they were the ones who did it instead of the Sovereignty.

    ## Deep Expanse Locations
    
    ### THE PALE REACHES
    The outermost regions of mapped space. Beyond B-rank standard patrol range. The Resonance Lattice behaves differently here — thinner in some areas, impossibly dense in others. The Pale (unranked entities) originate from somewhere in this region. No expedition sent to find their origin point has returned.
    
    **Hazard Classification:** OMEGA (Sovereignty designation for locations where rank-based protections do not apply normally)
    
    **Key Features:**
    - **The Signal Corridor** — A 40-light-year stretch where a repeating signal has been broadcasting for at least 2,000 years. No one has decoded it. The Aethon refuse to discuss it.
    - **The Collapse Zones** — Regions where the Resonance Lattice has catastrophically failed. Physical laws become unreliable. Ships passing through report crew members developing spontaneous ability manifestations — or losing the ones they had.
    - **The Architect's Scar** — A region of space where instruments detect what appears to be a massive scar in the Lattice itself. As if something the size of a solar system was violently removed. The Architect has never responded to queries about it.
    
    ### THE RUIN FIELDS
    Ancient structures scattered throughout the Expanse. Pre-System civilization remnants. No consistent architectural language — they were not all built by the same civilization.
    
    **The Six Major Ruins (Named):**
    1. **The Ossuary of Keth'Vor** — A station-sized structure built entirely from compressed biological material. Still produces heat. 
    2. **The Lattice Engine** — A device the size of a moon that appears designed to modify the Resonance Lattice directly. Non-functional. Every faction has attempted to activate it.
    3. **The Cartographer's Library** — Contains star charts for 40,000 systems not in any Sovereignty database. Most of the charted systems, when visited, are empty. Some are not.
    4. **The Throne Without Sovereign** — A single room inside an otherwise featureless asteroid. The room contains a chair. Anything that sits in the chair hears the Endless Dark.
    5. **The Graveyard of Giants** — 847 vessels, each the size of a continent, drifting in formation. No life signs. No damage. No explanation.
    6. **The Mirror Station** — A facility that exactly replicates whatever station is nearest to it. Currently adjacent to Crucible Station. The replica is perfect down to the inhabitants — except they are all dead.

    # PART FOUR: DUNGEONS & ENCOUNTER ZONES
    
    ## Dungeon Design Philosophy
    In VOID ASCENDANT, "dungeons" are not simply underground corridors. They are any bounded environment where the normal rules of civilization do not apply — places where rank advancement trials occur, where ancient tech goes active, where the Void bleeds through. They range from urban choke points to ancient ruins to the interior of living entities.
    
    ---
    
    ## DUNGEON: THE SUBLEVEL WARREN
    **Location:** Beneath any major Covenant city
    **Threat Level:** F-rank entry, D-rank recommended
    **Recommended Party Size:** 3–6
    **Primary Hazards:** Criminal factions, malfunctioning tech, collapsed infrastructure, occasional Lattice-dead zones (abilities suppressed)
    
    **Overview:**
    The infrastructure maintenance tunnels beneath Covenant cities. Officially, they are utility corridors. Unofficially, they are the Sovereignty's forgotten underbelly — home to unlicensed residents, black-market operations, and occasionally something that crawled up from deeper. Every city has them. None of them are fully mapped.
    
    **Level Structure:**
    - **Surface Layer (SL-1):** Active utility conduits. Patrols twice per standard cycle. Minor criminal activity. Low threat.
    - **Mid-Level (SL-2 to SL-5):** Where unlicensed populations live. Complex social hierarchies. The Covenant doesn't enter without significant force.
    - **Deep Level (SL-6 to SL-9):** Abandoned sectors. Structural instability. Relic tech from pre-System era. Something has been moving in SL-8 for the past three months. No one has confirmed what.
    - **The Sub-Dark (SL-10+):** Below the official plans. The Resonance Lattice is wrong here. Thick. Like wading through it. Entities that emerge from here are not in the System's database.
    
    **Key Encounter Nodes:**
    1. **The Broken Gate** — A deactivated security checkpoint. Someone has manually welded it open. Beyond it: SL-7. Cause of weld: unknown.
    2. **The Market Below** — SL-3. 200+ stalls. Everything from bootleg rank-advancement supplements (dangerous, ~40% effective) to weapons without serial numbers to information. The market boss: a C-rank former Covenant enforcer who defected.
    3. **The Lattice Pool** — SL-8. A location where Resonance has pooled physically — it's visible as a faintly luminous liquid collecting in a low point. Drinking it has unpredictable effects. Bathing in it has caused two documented spontaneous rank advancements and one documented dissolution of self.
    4. **The Archive Room** — SL-9. Someone has been collecting records here for a very long time. The records predate the System. The handwriting matches no known species.

    ## DUNGEON: THE PALE INCURSION ZONE
    **Location:** Any frontier territory; specific instance: Sector 7, Fringe Zone
    **Threat Level:** B-rank minimum. A-rank recommended.
    **Recommended Party Size:** 5–8
    **Primary Hazards:** The Pale (entities), Lattice degradation, reality anchor failure, Succession resonance bleed
    
    **Overview:**
    When the Pale breach into normal space, they do not simply attack. They *change* the space they occupy. The Resonance Lattice begins to degrade. Technological systems built on Resonance principles fail or behave unexpectedly. The affected zone becomes progressively more hostile to ranked individuals the longer the Pale remain.
    
    **Stage Progression (Incursion develops over time):**
    - **Stage 1 (Hours 0–6):** Pale Scouts. C-rank equivalent. Lattice slightly distorted. Tech functions normally.
    - **Stage 2 (Hours 6–24):** Pale Hunters. B-rank equivalent. Lattice noticeably distorted. 20% ability failure rate.
    - **Stage 3 (Days 1–3):** Pale Shapers. Attempting to restructure local space. A-rank threat equivalent. 50% ability failure rate. Physical reality begins to exhibit minor anomalies (gravity fluctuation, light behavior changes).
    - **Stage 4 (Days 3+):** Pale Sovereign. Single entity. S-rank equivalent. The zone is now Pale-space. Standard combat doctrine has no recommended response. Evacuation protocol only.
    
    **Key Encounter Nodes:**
    1. **The Breach Point** — Where they came through. Closing it requires either A-rank reality-anchor deployment or a Succession bloodline individual (their affinity can touch what opened it). The Covenant will never admit this.
    2. **The Changed Zone** — Area already restructured by Stage 3 Pale. Physical laws are suggestions here. Anything killed here does not stay dead unless the breach is closed first.
    3. **The Pale Shrine** — A structure the Pale have built or revealed. It resonates on a frequency below the Lattice. Standing next to it causes vivid memories of things that have not happened yet.

    ## DUNGEON: THE DOMINION BLACK SITE
    **Location:** Gray Station 7, Dominion Space
    **Threat Level:** C-rank entry, B-rank recommended
    **Recommended Party Size:** 4–6
    **Primary Hazards:** Elite Dominion security, experimental weapon systems, test subjects
    
    **Overview:**
    The Dominion does not officially conduct human experimentation. Their Black Sites conduct *enhanced rank-advancement research* — a distinction that the individuals inside would dispute. These stations are heavily secured, poorly mapped, and contain things the Dominion does not want extracted.
    
    **Facility Structure:**
    - **Exterior Docking/Security:** 40 Dominion Iron Guard (C-rank minimum). Two automated defense systems. Biometric locks keyed to senior Dominion personnel only.
    - **Research Level Alpha:** Standard weapons R&D. Relatively normal. The scientists here know they work for a weapons manufacturer. They're fine with it.
    - **Research Level Beta:** Where enhanced subjects are kept. Individuals who underwent experimental rank-advancement procedures. Some succeeded, gaining capabilities outside the System's standard parameters. Most did not. The ones who didn't are still alive, which is part of what makes Beta complicated.
    - **Research Level Omega:** Classified. The official manifest says it doesn't exist. The power consumption data says otherwise. It consumes more energy than the rest of the station combined.

    # PART FIVE: THE EXTENDED BESTIARY
    
    ## Classification System
    All entities are classified along three axes:
    - **Rank Equivalent:** F through S (measures threat to a single ranked individual)
    - **Lattice Relationship:** Positive (uses Resonance), Neutral (ignores it), Negative (consumes/degrades it), Void (exists below the Lattice)
    - **Origin:** Natural (evolved), Constructed (built/engineered), Emergent (arose from Lattice phenomena), Ancient (predates the System)
    
    ---
    
    ## STANDARD THREAT ENTITIES
    
    ### THE PALE
    **Rank Equivalent:** Scout: C | Hunter: B | Shaper: A | Sovereign: S+
    **Lattice Relationship:** Negative (active degradation)
    **Origin:** Unknown. Possibly Ancient. Possibly something else entirely.
    
    The Pale are the most dangerous recurring threat in the known galaxy. They are not animals. They are not intelligent in any conventional sense — they do not negotiate, do not flee, do not respond to any ranked ability in a predictable way. They are white. Not albino — white in a way that suggests the absence of color is a choice, or a property, rather than a biological trait. Their forms vary: some are vaguely humanoid, some are geometric, some are large enough to engulf a starship. They share one consistent feature: the Resonance Lattice dies around them.
    
    **Combat Notes:**
    - Standard ranked abilities have reduced effect (30–70% depending on Pale tier and Lattice degradation in area)
    - Physical weapons function normally; energy weapons powered by Resonance systems may fail
    - Pale Hunters exhibit tactical awareness; they will identify the highest-ranked individual in a group and focus
    - Pale Shapers do not attack directly; they restructure the environment to make combat impossible
    - The only confirmed effective counter above B-rank: Succession bloodline abilities, which operate below the Lattice
    
    **Behavior Notes:**
    They do not eat. They do not appear to reproduce in any observed manner. They breach from somewhere. They do whatever they're doing. They leave or they don't. No Pale has ever been captured alive — not because they fight to the death, but because containment systems consistently fail in their proximity.
    
    ---
    
    ### VOID-SCARRED
    **Rank Equivalent:** D to A (varies dramatically based on base rank and degree of scarring)
    **Lattice Relationship:** Negative (uncontrolled emission)
    **Origin:** Emergent — ranked individuals whose System Chip has been damaged or corrupted
    
    Void-Scarred are ranked individuals whose connection to the Resonance Lattice has become unstable. Their System Chip no longer regulates their abilities properly. The result is a being that emits Resonance uncontrollably, burning through its own biological systems in the process. They are not undead. They are dying, very slowly, while becoming increasingly dangerous.
    
    **Stages of Scarring:**
    - **Stage 1 (Marked):** Visible Resonance bleed from the System Chip site. Abilities fluctuate. Personality changes — increased aggression, reduced impulse control. Still rational. Still capable of conversation.
    - **Stage 2 (Burning):** Resonance emissions visible at all times. Uncontrolled ability discharges. Rationality compromised. Will attack ranked individuals instinctively — their degraded chip reads all active Resonance as a threat.
    - **Stage 3 (Fallen):** No rational function remains. Body is partially dissolved by uncontained Resonance. What remains is an ability-emission engine with enough biological structure to move and seek targets. The original person is gone.
    
    **Notable:** The Covenant classifies Void-Scarred as a public health threat and authorizes lethal removal at all stages. The Covenant does not publicize how many Stage 1 Scarred exist in the Deep levels of its cities, living quietly, managing their condition, terrified of being reported.

    ### RESONANCE FAUNA — EXPANSE MEGABEASTS
    
    #### THE LATTICE LEVIATHAN
    **Rank Equivalent:** S
    **Lattice Relationship:** Positive (draws from ambient Resonance)
    **Origin:** Natural (evolved in Expanse deep space)
    **Size:** 40–200 km in length
    
    The apex predator of the Expanse. Not a ship. Not a construct. A living organism that evolved in the void of space, feeding on concentrated Resonance nodes. Their biology is unlike anything evolved in atmosphere — no bones, no conventional organs, a distributed nervous system that is 40% Resonance-active tissue. They move by directly manipulating local space using their Resonance organs. They are not aggressive toward ships unless the ship is emitting a strong Resonance signature — which puts all ranked individuals at risk.
    
    **The Problem:** An S-rank individual's full-power ability output can attract a Lattice Leviathan from 30 light-minutes away.
    
    **Combat Notes (for the extremely committed):**
    - Hull weapons (non-Resonance-based) deal normal damage
    - A coordinated fleet action has killed Leviathans twice in recorded history
    - Their defensive response — a Resonance pulse — can damage or destroy System Chips within 2 km
    - Succession abilities may allow communication (one documented incident; the record is sealed)
    
    ---
    
    #### THE VOID LAMPREY
    **Rank Equivalent:** B
    **Lattice Relationship:** Void (below Lattice)
    **Origin:** Ancient
    **Size:** 3–12 meters
    
    A predator that exists partially in the space below the Resonance Lattice. It can shift between material space and the space between the Lattice and the Endless Dark at will. Conventional weapons pass through it when it is in its shifted state. Resonance-based weapons are ineffective — it feeds on them. The only reliable counter: abilities that touch the Void layer, or Succession blood.
    
    **Appearance:** When visible, a cylindrical organism covered in sensory filaments, with a radial mouth that opens to six segments. When feeding, its entire body becomes faintly luminous. Beautiful in a deeply alarming way.
    
    **Habitat:** Anywhere the Lattice is thin. They congregate in Collapse Zones. Occasionally appear in lower levels of Covenant cities where the infrastructure has damaged the Lattice over decades of energy extraction.
    
    ---
    
    #### THE ECHO SWARM
    **Rank Equivalent:** C (individual) | A (full swarm)
    **Lattice Relationship:** Positive (mimicry)
    **Origin:** Emergent
    **Size:** Individual 0.3 meters; swarm 10–500 meters (combined)
    
    Single organisms of no particular threat. In groups of 200+, they develop emergent behavior: collective intelligence, coordinated attacks, and — most alarmingly — Resonance ability mimicry. An Echo Swarm that has observed a specific ability being used can produce a crude approximation of that ability using coordinated biological action.
    
    **The Tactical Problem:** An Echo Swarm that has observed an S-rank ability demonstration can produce a B-rank equivalent of that ability. And there are a lot of them.

    ## CONSTRUCTED ENTITIES
    
    ### SOVEREIGNTY ENFORCER UNITS (SEUs)
    **Rank Equivalent:** D to B (model dependent)
    **Lattice Relationship:** Positive (Resonance-powered)
    **Origin:** Constructed
    
    The Sovereignty's frontline security automation. Not fully autonomous — each unit operates under a handler's oversight. Built with integrated Resonance weapons systems, structural durability rated against C-rank physical impacts, and a target acquisition AI updated quarterly.
    
    **Models:**
    - **SEU-3 (Standard):** D-rank equivalent. Mass deployed. Crowd control.
    - **SEU-7 (Combat):** C-rank equivalent. Deployed for anti-insurgency. The primary tool of Covenant enforcement in Deep Level operations.
    - **SEU-ALPHA (Elite):** B-rank equivalent. Only deployed on direct Council authorization. Each unit costs more than a mid-tier warship.
    - **PHANTOM Unit (Classified):** Rumors only. Allegedly A-rank equivalent. No confirmed sightings. Three individuals who claimed confirmed sightings are unavailable for comment.

    ### WRAITH-CLASS CONSTRUCTS
    **Rank Equivalent:** Varies (classification pending)
    **Lattice Relationship:** Anomalous (below standard Lattice signatures)
    **Origin:** Unknown
    
    WRAITH — the intelligence embedded in Ren Voss's System Chip — is not the only one of its kind. It is, however, the only one that is confirmed functional and communicative. Others have been found: in ruins, in decommissioned Covenant deep-research stations, in one case inside the Sovereignty Council's own sealed archives. None of the others responded to query. WRAITH has not explained why it is different.
    
    What is known: WRAITH-class constructs are not built on Resonance principles. They operate on something else. The Architect does not acknowledge them when queried. This is notable because the Architect acknowledges everything.

    # PART SIX: VEHICLES & VESSELS
    
    ## Ship Classification System
    
    All vessels in Sovereignty space are classified by the System's own framework — which assigns a Combat Power rating (CP) to ships the same way it assigns ranks to individuals. This is both practically useful and philosophically interesting.
    
    ### VESSEL CLASSES
    
    #### SKIFF CLASS (CP: 10–50)
    **Crew:** 1–4
    **Length:** 15–40 meters
    **FTL Capable:** No (standard); Modified skiffs with jury-rigged FTL exist but are illegal and dangerous
    **Primary Use:** In-system transport, short-range patrol, personal craft
    
    The working vehicle of the galaxy. Every docking station has hundreds. They are to starships what motorcycles are to vehicles — fast, exposed, and not rated for anything serious.
    
    **Notable Variants:**
    - *Scrap Runner* — The standard unlicensed version used in Deep Level operations. No transponder. Maximum interior cargo space. Zero comfort.
    - *Covenant Interceptor Skiff* — The fast-pursuit variant used by Covenant enforcement. Rated for C-rank operator; has Resonance-boosted engines that respond to the pilot's rank signature.
    - *The Ghost* — A custom variant. Coated in signal-absorbing material. No Resonance signature. Used by intelligence operatives and extremely paranoid individuals.
    
    ---
    
    #### FRIGATE CLASS (CP: 200–800)
    **Crew:** 40–200
    **Length:** 150–400 meters
    **FTL Capable:** Yes (standard jump drive)
    **Primary Use:** Patrol, escort, fast attack
    
    The bread-and-butter of Sovereignty military presence. A well-crewed frigate with a B-rank captain is a significant threat to anything short of a capital ship. The Dominion's frigate variants are notably superior in raw firepower; the Covenant's are superior in longevity and Resonance-integrated systems.
    
    **Notable Variants:**
    - *Sovereignty Lancer-Class* — The standard military frigate. Resonance-integrated targeting, crew quarters rated for 90, three main weapon batteries.
    - *Dominion Hammer-Class* — Pure firepower. Minimal crew comfort. The Dominion believes comfort is an enemy of readiness.
    - *Keth Warspear-Class* — Smaller, faster, built for boarding actions. Keth naval doctrine emphasizes getting personnel aboard enemy ships over long-range engagement.
    - *Crucible Free Frigate* — No standard design. Modified civilian vessels armed to frigate-equivalent rating. Variable but often surprisingly effective.
    
    ---
    
    #### CRUISER CLASS (CP: 1,500–5,000)
    **Crew:** 500–2,000
    **Length:** 600–1,200 meters
    **FTL Capable:** Yes (jump drive + one emergency micro-jump per 12 hours)
    **Primary Use:** Fleet backbone, extended operations, force projection
    
    A cruiser can operate independently for six months without resupply. It is the minimum vessel class for a Sovereignty Admiral's flag. A single cruiser in orbit can engage in sustained planetary bombardment sufficient to sterilize a continent.
    
    ---
    
    #### CAPITAL CLASS (CP: 10,000+)
    **Crew:** 5,000–80,000
    **Length:** 2,000–40,000 meters
    **FTL Capable:** Yes (dedicated jump drive; can create micro-FTL bubbles for escorted vessels)
    **Primary Use:** Fleet command, theater-level force projection, orbital siege
    
    Only seven capital-class vessels exist in the known galaxy. Each one is effectively a city. Each one has a name. Each one has a history. When a capital-class ship appears in a system, the politics of that system change.
    
    **Named Capital Vessels:**
    1. *The Sovereign Will* — Sovereignty Council flagship. 38,000 meters. Never been in direct combat. Its presence alone has ended three wars.
    2. *The Iron Absolute* — Dominion capital ship. 28,000 meters. Has been in direct combat seventeen times. Shows it.
    3. *The Nameless* — Registration unknown. Last documented sighting: Expanse fringe, forty years ago. Believed destroyed. Believed.
    4. *The First Covenant* — The oldest vessel of its class. 4,100 years old. Pre-System. Still functional. The Sovereignty does not know how.
    
    ---
    
    ## GROUND VEHICLES
    
    ### RESONANCE CYCLES
    Personal transport. A standard Resonance Cycle operates by generating a low-level Resonance field beneath the rider, providing frictionless movement at up to 300 km/h on flat terrain. They are keyed to their owner's System Chip signature — a stolen cycle is a useless cycle. High-end models can be modified for riders with specific ability types, acting as an amplifier platform during movement.
    
    ### IRON CRAWLERS
    Heavy ground transport. Used by the Keth as their primary military ground vehicle. Articulated tracks, hull rated against B-rank physical impacts, crew of 6–10. The Keth version has an open fighting platform on top — they consider enclosed fighting positions to be a sign of poor character.
    
    ### THE SOVEREIGN'S BLADE (Vehicle)
    A specialized single-operator combat platform used by S-rank Sovereignty enforcers. Not a vehicle in the traditional sense — it's a wearable frame that amplifies the operator's abilities by routing power through the System Chip. An S-rank individual in a Sovereign's Blade is classified as a strategic asset equivalent to a frigate.

    # PART SEVEN: WEAPONS & TECHNOLOGY
    
    ## Weapon Categories
    
    ### RESONANCE WEAPONS
    The dominant technology of the Sovereignty. Weapons that use the Resonance Lattice as their operating medium.
    
    **Type I — Resonance Pulse Emitters:**
    Standard ranged weapon. Generates a directed pulse of Resonance energy. Effect on target depends on their rank: F-rank target receiving a B-rank pulse will be reduced to component matter. B-rank target receiving the same pulse will experience reduced ability function for 4–8 hours. A-rank targets are minimally affected. S-rank targets must be hit with S-rank Resonance weapons, which require S-rank operators.
    
    **Type II — Lattice Disruptors:**
    Specialized weapons designed to damage the Resonance connection between a target and the Lattice. Effectively a weapon designed to disable rank-based abilities without necessarily killing. Considered "humane" by Covenant doctrine. Considered deeply threatening by anyone who relies on their abilities for everything.
    
    **Type III — Resonance Blades:**
    Melee weapons with integrated Resonance channels. A Resonance Blade concentrates the wielder's ability output through the blade edge, allowing rank-commensurate cutting capability against rank-hardened targets. An A-rank warrior with an A-class Resonance Blade can cut through materials that would stop a standard frigate's broadside.
    
    **Type IV — Void Weapons (Restricted):**
    Weapons that operate below the Resonance Lattice. Developed by capturing and studying Succession ability output. Classified by the Sovereignty at the highest level. Officially do not exist. A Void Weapon affects targets regardless of rank — the A-rank hardening that protects against standard weapons does not apply. Three confirmed examples exist. All three are in Sovereignty Council custody. Two of them work.
    
    ---
    
    ### CONVENTIONAL WEAPONS
    Technology predating the System. Still effective against unranked individuals and low-rank targets. Irrelevant against A-rank and above under standard conditions.
    
    **Kinetic Accelerators (Railguns):** Magnetically accelerated projectiles. High mass, high velocity. Against F and E-rank targets, lethal. Against D-rank and above with active ability reinforcement, negligible. Against S-rank, genuinely irrelevant. However — kinetic weapons work in Lattice-dead zones where Resonance weapons fail. This is their primary tactical value.
    
    **Chemical Propellant Weapons:** Archaic. The Keth still use them in ceremonial contexts and keep functional versions because they work in environments that destroy Resonance-based systems. Practical wisdom dressed as tradition.

    ## TECHNOLOGY SYSTEMS
    
    ### THE SYSTEM CHIP
    The fundamental technology of Sovereignty civilization. A sub-centimeter biotech implant placed at the base of the skull during the ranking ceremony. It:
    - Interfaces directly with the nervous system and the Resonance Lattice
    - Administers rank advancement protocols (testing, verification, advancement)
    - Regulates ability output (prevents involuntary ability discharge in standard conditions)
    - Provides the System's UI (the floating display panels, rank information, ability descriptions visible to the individual)
    - Serves as personal identification, financial account, communication device, and health monitor
    
    **Vulnerabilities:**
    - Can be damaged by Void-origin attacks
    - Can be corrupted by sustained Lattice disruption (Void-Scarred condition)
    - Can be hacked (theoretically; in practice requires S-rank technical ability or Succession-level Void access)
    - Ceases to function in areas where the Lattice is completely absent
    
    **WRAITH Exception:** Ren Voss's System Chip hosts WRAITH, an intelligence that operates independently of the Lattice. This should not be possible. The Architect has not commented.
    
    ---
    
    ### FASTER-THAN-LIGHT TRAVEL (JUMP DRIVE)
    FTL travel in the VOID ASCENDANT universe operates through the Resonance Lattice itself. A Jump Drive generates a Resonance bubble that temporarily places the vessel in the Lattice layer — where distances are compressed relative to material space — and then re-emerges at a calculated destination.
    
    **Mechanics:**
    - Jump range is limited by drive rating and available Resonance energy
    - Jump accuracy decreases with distance; long jumps require navigational corrections
    - Cannot jump within a planetary gravity well (minimum safe distance: 4 planetary radii)
    - Cannot jump inside Lattice-dead zones or Pale-incursion areas
    - Jump drives are rank-rated: a B-rank drive operated by an A-rank pilot performs at A-rank parameters
    
    **Void Jump (Classified):** The Succession has theorized a method of transit through the Endless Dark rather than the Lattice. It would have no range limits and no Lattice dependency. It would also require the navigator to touch the Endless Dark directly. The Covenant considers this line of research approximately as alarming as it sounds.
    
    ---
    
    ### RESONANCE COMMUNICATION (RESCOM)
    The standard communication technology. ResCom transmissions travel through the Lattice at speeds far exceeding light — galactic-scale communication with acceptable delays (minutes to hours depending on distance). In Lattice-dead zones, ResCom fails completely. In Pale-incursion areas, ResCom becomes unreliable. In the Endless Dark, there is no ResCom, but things hear you anyway.
    
    ---
    
    ### THE CARTOGRAPHER NETWORK
    An AI-administered navigation system that maintains positional data for all registered vessels and jump destinations throughout Sovereignty space. Run by a non-ranked intelligence (one of the few major AIs in regular use — the Sovereignty is cautious about AI following an incident 200 years ago that the records describe only as "the Cartographer's Error" and do not elaborate on).
