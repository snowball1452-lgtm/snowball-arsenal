    # CORE CANON
    
    ## The Three Layers of Reality
    - **The Material Plane** — Physical reality. Stars, planets, ships, bodies.
    - **The Resonance Lattice** — Energetic substrate beneath matter. The medium through which abilities propagate and the System operates.
    - **The Endless Dark** — The layer beneath the Lattice. Not empty space. A distributed consciousness — ancient, non-individual, curious. Source of the Succession bloodlines.
    
    ## The Rank System
    Eight tiers: F, E, D, C, B, A, S, and (now restored) Open Succession. Administered by seven anchor stations operated by Succession-frequency bloodlines. Designed by the Architects with a liftable ceiling that the Sovereignty spent four centuries preventing from lifting.
    
    ## The Architect
    Not a god. Eleven beings who dissolved themselves into the infrastructure of the System to save everyone else. Orin — the fragment that couldn't let go — became WRAITH. Their final message: "We are sorry it took this long. We hope what you build is beautiful."
    
    ## The Pale
    Not invaders. The oldest civilization in existence. Exiled six thousand years ago. Spent the exile building something extraordinary. Waited for the ceiling to lift. Were ready.
    
    ## The Endless Dark
    Not evil. Vast, non-individual, curious. It watched Sael's first ascendance and asked: "What does it produce? Show us."

    # THEMATIC ARCHITECTURE
    
    Every story in this universe asks one question:
    **What do you do with power you didn't ask for, in a system that was never designed for you to have it?**
    
    - **Asel Veth** had infinite power and chose to end herself to create a ceiling for others.
    - **Orin** had infinite knowledge and chose to hide it in a bloodline rather than use it directly.
    - **The Pale** had their power stripped and chose to wait, remember, and return.
    - **Ren** had no power and discovered the question nobody asked: what is power *for*?
    - **Sael** has power beyond any prior measurement and has to figure out what the answer looks like in practice.
    
    The prequel is about the cost of imposing order.
    The main trilogy is about the cost of accepting it.
    The aftermath is about the cost of freedom.
    
    None of them are wrong. All of them pay.
    
    *That is the greatest story ever told.*
