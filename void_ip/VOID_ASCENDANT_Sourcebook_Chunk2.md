    # PART FOUR: THE KNOWN GALAXY — PLANETARY SYSTEMS & LOCATIONS
    
    ## The Sovereignty's Domain: Mapped Space
    
    The Aethon Sovereignty controls approximately 4,200 star systems across three galactic arms. Of these, roughly 1,800 are fully colonized, 900 are resource-extraction operations, 400 are contested or border territories, and the remaining 1,100 are classified — most under Void Research Directorate seal.
    
    ---
    
    ## CORE WORLDS — Tier 1 Planetary Systems
    
    ### AETHON PRIME
    **Classification:** Capital World — Class 1 Sovereign Territory  
    **Star Type:** G-type main sequence (Sol-analog)  
    **Population:** 28.4 billion  
    **Gravity:** 1.0G standard  
    **Atmosphere:** Nitrogen-oxygen, Type I breathable  
    **Climate:** Engineered — orbital climate arrays maintain perpetual temperate zones across all landmasses  
    
    **Description:** The seat of the Sovereignty. Aethon Prime is not a natural world — it has been so extensively terraformed and re-engineered over four millennia that its original geology is largely inaccessible below 40km of urban and industrial layering. The surface is divided into six continental Echelons, each administering a specific function of empire.
    
    **The Six Echelons:**
    - **The Crown Echelon** — The Sovereign's seat. A 300km plateau of pure white Resonance-crystal towers, accessible only to A-rank and above without escort. The Sovereign's palace is the tallest structure in the known galaxy at 18km.
    - **The War Echelon** — Military command. The Grand Marshal's headquarters. Fleet coordination. The Ranking Bureau's central server array. 
    - **The Covenant Echelon** — The Iron Covenant's administrative hub. Libraries, Archives, the Grand Codex, the Bureau of Records.
    - **The Trade Echelon** — Commerce, manufacturing, the Merchant Guilds. Most humans and Velhari live here.
    - **The Research Echelon** — Science, augmentation clinics, the Augmentation Bureau, medical research. Access restricted above D-rank research personnel.
    - **The Undercity** — Everything beneath the official surface. 40km deep. Unranked majority. Black markets. Void cult activity. Where Ren Voss was born.
    
    **Resonance Density:** Extreme. The Lattice is visibly luminescent at night in the Crown Echelon — rank advancement happens 30% faster on Aethon Prime than the galactic average.
    
    **Key Locations:**
    - *The Grand Codex* — A library containing every piece of recorded knowledge in Sovereignty history. 900 floors. The deepest 300 are classified.
    - *The Ranking Bureau Central* — Where rank chips are manufactured, calibrated, and monitored. Ren's chip was installed here.
    - *The Undercity Market Ring* — 200km circumference black market. Every illegal thing in the galaxy passes through here.
    - *The Void Gate* — A sealed location beneath the Research Echelon. What is behind it is not officially acknowledged.
    
    ---
    
    ### VELHAR
    **Classification:** Tier 1 Core World  
    **Star Type:** K-type (slightly cooler, orange-tinted light)  
    **Population:** 18.7 billion  
    **Gravity:** 1.2G  
    **Atmosphere:** Nitrogen-oxygen, slightly elevated CO2. Natives have denser lung tissue.  
    **Climate:** Arid continental interiors, dense jungle coastal zones  
    
    **Description:** Homeworld of the Velhari species. The planet's higher gravity produces stockier, denser life forms. The jungles of the coastal Vel Strips are among the most biodiverse environments in the Sovereignty — and also the source of the majority of the galaxy's pharmaceutical compounds. The Covenant maintains extensive botanic research stations here. The interior deserts are dotted with ancient Velhari ruin-cities that predate the System by at least 6,000 years.
    
    **Key Locations:**
    - *The Vel Strip Research Stations (1-7)* — Covenant-run pharmaceutical research. Strip 4 was destroyed 200 years ago in an incident the Covenant has never formally explained.
    - *The Ruin Cities* — Pre-System Velhari civilization. Architecture uses geometric principles that modern Resonance engineers cannot fully replicate. The ruins resonate differently than anything else on the planet.
    - *The Velhari Deep Archive* — Buried beneath the largest ruin city. Contains Velhari records the Sovereignty has classified. The Velhari know. They don't talk about it openly.
    
    ---
    
    ### KETH'SARA
    **Classification:** Tier 1 Core World — Joint Sovereignty/Covenant Administration  
    **Star Type:** Binary — G-type and M-type in close orbit  
    **Population:** 9.1 billion  
    **Gravity:** 0.85G  
    **Atmosphere:** Nitrogen-oxygen, trace noble gases produce slight atmospheric luminescence at dawn and dusk  
    **Climate:** Temperate to sub-arctic. Two distinct seasonal cycles due to binary star orbital mechanics.  
    
    **Description:** The spiritual center of the Covenant's philosophical tradition. Keth'Sara's unusual atmospheric chemistry produces dawn and dusk skies in shades of deep violet and amber simultaneously — a phenomenon called the Double Veil that has been considered sacred by the planet's inhabitants for millennia. The Covenant established its oldest monastery here 3,800 years ago. The planet's low gravity enables architectural styles impossible elsewhere — needle-spires of Resonance-glass rising 4-5km without structural reinforcement.
    
    **Key Locations:**
    - *The First Monastery* — The oldest continuously inhabited building in Covenant territory. 3,800 years old. The Grandmaster of the Covenant maintains an office (though rarely occupies it) here.
    - *The Archives of the Veil* — Covenant historical records predating the System. Partially open to accredited researchers.
    - *The Resonance Gardens* — A 400km² natural park where the Lattice naturally forms visible patterns in low-light conditions. Used for rank advancement meditation.

    ## FRONTIER WORLDS — The Expanse
    
    The Expanse is the term for the outer systems — star systems at the edge of Sovereignty-mapped space where central authority is thin, infrastructure is sparse, and the rules are enforced by whoever has the most guns and the highest rank.
    
    ### CRUCIBLE STATION (Chapters 7–12 Setting)
    **Classification:** Deep Space Station — Class 3 Frontier Installation  
    **Location:** The Calyx Expanse, approximately 40 light-years beyond the last Tier-2 system  
    **Population:** ~220,000 permanent residents, ~80,000 transient  
    **Gravity:** 0.6G (station rotation, inner rings 0.8G)  
    **Atmosphere:** Recycled, Type II breathable. Slight metallic tang that longtime residents stop noticing.  
    
    **Description:** Crucible Station is the largest permanent structure in the Calyx Expanse. Originally constructed as a mining relay station 800 years ago, it has grown through 300 years of unplanned expansion into a sprawling orbital city that looks — from the outside — like three asteroids welded together with a city growing on the seams. The Sovereignty technically administers it. In practice, the station runs itself through a council of the seven largest trading houses, and rank law is enforced only when convenient.
    
    **Station Rings (Interior to Exterior):**
    - *The Core Ring* — Power generation. Water recycling. Life support. The station's nervous system. Access restricted — only engineers and ranking security.
    - *The Commerce Ring* — Markets, trading floors, warehouses. The economic heart.
    - *The Residence Ring* — Housing, schools, medical bays. Where the 220,000 permanent residents live.
    - *The Outer Dock* — Shipyards, fuel depots, cargo processing. Constant activity. The loudest part of the station at any hour.
    - *The Under-Rings* — The unofficial rings. Three levels beneath the official station map. Unlit corridors. Factions operate here.
    
    **Key Factions at Crucible:**
    - *The Seven Houses* — Trading families who've controlled the Commerce Ring for generations
    - *The Drift Wardens* — Ex-military security force, nominally neutral, actually on House payroll
    - *The Hollow* — Unranked community organization. Part mutual aid, part militia. Ren finds allies here.
    - *Void Cult Cell 7* — Small cell of the Succession operating under cover as a shipping concern

    ### VETH (The Ghost World)
    **Classification:** Contested — Sovereignty Claimed, Succession Disputed  
    **Star Type:** White dwarf remnant — the star died 2,000 years ago  
    **Population:** Unknown — estimated 200,000 to 2 million  
    **Gravity:** 0.7G  
    **Atmosphere:** Thin. Requires breathing apparatus for prolonged surface exposure.  
    **Climate:** Frozen tundra. Average surface temperature -80°C. Subsurface city network.  
    
    **Description:** Veth was a thriving world before its star died. The population refused to evacuate. They built down instead — 400 years of subsurface construction produced a city network that now extends 12km beneath the surface, heated by geothermal taps and lit by artificial sun-arrays. The people of Veth are technically Sovereignty citizens but functionally independent. They pay no taxes. They acknowledge no governor. Their rank system operates under different rules that nobody in the Sovereignty has been able to fully audit.
    
    **Why Veth Matters:**  
    The Succession has deep roots here. The long darkness after the star died produced a culture that views the Endless Dark not as a threat but as a neighbor. The ghost-lights visible in Veth's subsurface corridors are not explained by the Lattice. They are something else.

    ### THE DEAD SYSTEMS
    **Classification:** Quarantine — Access Prohibited under Sovereignty Statute 7-Null  
    **Count:** 47 confirmed dead systems  
    
    **Description:** Systems where all life has been extinguished. Not destroyed — extinguished. The planets are intact. The infrastructure, in many cases, is intact. The people are simply gone. The System records in each dead system show rank advancement occurring normally up to a specific date — and then all records stop simultaneously.
    
    The Sovereignty's official explanation: catastrophic weapon events during the Pre-Covenant Wars. The Succession's explanation: the Architect ran a test, and those populations failed. The truth, which only the Grand Marshal and the Sovereign know, is that each dead system was a Void Gate activation that went wrong.

    # PART FIVE: FLORA & BOTANICAL SYSTEMS
    
    ## Classification Framework
    The Sovereignty uses a four-tier botanical classification system developed by the Covenant's Natural Philosophy division 1,200 years ago. The system classifies plants not only by biology but by their relationship to the Resonance Lattice.
    
    **Tier N (Null Flora):** No Lattice interaction. Standard biological organisms. ~60% of all known plant species.  
    **Tier L (Lattice Flora):** Passive Lattice interaction. Absorbs ambient Resonance, produces compounds with mild ability-enhancing or ability-suppressing properties. ~35% of known species.  
    **Tier R (Resonant Flora):** Active Lattice interaction. The plant has a measurable Resonance signature. Its biological processes influence the local Lattice density. ~4.5% of known species.  
    **Tier V (Void Flora):** Confirmed Endless Dark contamination. The plant's biological processes draw from below the Lattice. Extremely rare. All known specimens are under Sovereignty seal. ~0.5% of known species — though Succession researchers believe the true number is much higher and the Sovereignty is suppressing the data.

    ## KEY FLORA CATALOG
    
    ### RESONANT SPECIES
    
    **Lattice Bloom (Resonara crystallis)**  
    *Classification: Tier R*  
    *Native to: Keth'Sara, cultivated across Core Worlds*  
    *Appearance:* A medium shrub, 1–2m tall, with crystalline petals that refract light into rank-color spectrums. Blue petals for D-rank ambient areas. Red in S-rank zones. Black-white in areas of Void contamination.  
    *Properties:* The petals, when dried and ground, produce a powder that temporarily elevates Resonance sensitivity by 15–30%. Used in Covenant meditation practice. Controlled substance in Sovereignty jurisdiction — possession of more than 200g requires research license.  
    *Lore:* The Lattice Bloom is considered the Covenant's sacred plant. Covenant temples always maintain a living specimen. Older, larger specimens (100+ years) have been observed to move their petals without wind — tracking the nearest ranked individual as if watching.
    
    ---
    
    **Voidweed (Tenebris vulgaris)**  
    *Classification: Tier V — CONTROLLED*  
    *Native to: Unknown — specimens discovered across 12 systems, no common origin world identified*  
    *Appearance:* A dark, low-growing ground cover. Leaves are matte black that absorbs light abnormally. No flowers. No seeds found — reproduction mechanism unknown.  
    *Properties:* Proximity to Voidweed suppresses System readouts. A field of it can reduce apparent rank by 1–2 tiers. Direct contact with the sap produces hallucinations consistent with Void exposure — subjects report hearing "something old" and being shown "the time before the Architect."  
    *Lore:* The Sovereignty classifies all Voidweed specimens and destroys them on contact. The Succession cultivates it. There are rumors of a world somewhere in the Dead Systems that is entirely covered in it — where the System cannot read anything, and something very old has been living undisturbed for a very long time.
    
    ---
    
    **Ironbark (Ferroveritas stellaris)**  
    *Classification: Tier L*  
    *Native to: Multiple frontier worlds — extremely adaptable*  
    *Appearance:* A massive tree, 30–80m tall, trunk diameter 4–12m. Bark has iron-gray coloration with faint blue Lattice-light visible in growth rings when cut.  
    *Properties:* Ironbark wood is the most structurally sound organic material in the known galaxy — stronger than standard steel at equivalent weight. Frontier communities use it for building. The heartwood of very old trees (500+) contains crystallized Resonance deposits that function as natural ability amplifiers. These deposits are worth fortunes.  
    *Lore:* Ironbark groves are considered sacred by multiple frontier cultures. Cutting an Ironbark without ritual acknowledgment is considered deeply bad luck — and statistically, settlements that clearcut Ironbark groves without ceremony do experience anomalously high accident rates. Researchers attribute this to coincidence. Frontier inhabitants attribute it to something else.
    
    ---
    
    **Seraphwillow (Velhar coastal variant)**  
    *Classification: Tier R*  
    *Native to: Velhari coastal Vel Strips*  
    *Appearance:* A weeping-willow analog with silver-white trailing fronds up to 15m long. In darkness, the fronds emit pale blue luminescence. In the presence of high-ranked individuals, the luminescence shifts to match their System color.  
    *Properties:* The fronds contain compounds used in the most effective known healing accelerants. When processed correctly, Seraphwillow extract accelerates cellular regeneration by 400-800% — the basis of most top-tier medical treatment. Raw frond contact produces mild euphoria and temporary elevation of ENDURANCE stat.  
    *Lore:* The Seraphwillow is used in Covenant burial practice. The fronds are woven into burial shrouds. The belief is that the willow guides the Resonance of the dead gently back into the Lattice rather than allowing it to disperse chaotically. Whether this is metaphor or literal is a question Covenant theologians argue about.
    
    ---
    
    **Duskfruit (Velox nocturnus)**  
    *Classification: Tier L*  
    *Native to: Multiple mid-rim worlds*  
    *Appearance:* A vine-climbing plant producing dark purple fruit approximately fist-sized. Fruit glows faintly red when ripe.  
    *Properties:* The fruit is mildly toxic in large quantities and mildly stimulating in small ones — the primary recreational substance of the frontier. Dried and processed, it produces a compound ("Dusk") that temporarily suppresses fear response while enhancing SPEED and PERCEPTION by 10–20%. Widely used by low-rank frontier fighters before combat. Highly addictive. The Sovereignty regulates it; the frontier ignores the regulation.  
    *Lore:* "Dusk head" is a slang term for someone who's been using Duskfruit compound too long — recognizable by the faint red tint in their eyes and the way they flinch at things that aren't there.
    
    ---
    
    **Resonance Moss (Muscus resonantis)**  
    *Classification: Tier R*  
    *Native to: Cave systems, deep subsurface environments galaxy-wide*  
    *Appearance:* A thick carpet moss, blue-green, that grows on stone surfaces in environments with low light but high Lattice density. Produces a steady soft luminescence.  
    *Properties:* Resonance Moss creates localized Lattice amplification zones. Rank advancement in rooms covered with mature Resonance Moss occurs 50% faster. Extended exposure produces heightened Resonance sensitivity and, in some individuals, the first unlocking of dormant ability potential. The Covenant maintains Resonance Moss gardens specifically for this purpose.  
    *Lore:* The oldest Resonance Moss colonies in known space are in the cave systems of Keth'Sara. Some of the colony organisms are estimated to be over 2,000 years old. These ancient colonies don't just amplify the Lattice — they seem to remember it. Covenant researchers report that meditation in proximity to ancient colonies produces "memories that are not theirs."

    # PART SIX: FAUNA & CREATURE CATALOG
    
    ## Classification Framework
    **Class I (Standard Fauna):** No Lattice interaction. Conventional biological organisms. Majority of animal life.  
    **Class II (Lattice-Sensitive Fauna):** Passive Lattice sensitivity. Reacts to ranked individuals. Can detect ability use. Used extensively in security and tracking.  
    **Class III (Resonant Fauna):** Active Lattice abilities. Creatures with genuine rank-analog capability. Treated under Sovereignty law as quasi-ranked beings with limited protections.  
    **Class IV (Void Fauna):** Contaminated by or native to the Endless Dark. Officially does not exist. Actually exists. The Void Research Directorate has files.

    ## KEY CREATURE CATALOG
    
    ### VOID-CLASS CREATURES
    
    **The Hollow Ones**  
    *Classification: Class IV — Sovereignty Denial Level: Total*  
    *Known Locations: Dead Systems, Veth subsurface, Void Gates (post-activation)*  
    *Appearance:* Varies. The Hollow Ones do not have a fixed form — they inhabit the corpses or structures of whatever they encounter and animate them. A Hollow One might appear as a dead soldier walking, a collapsed building that moves, or simply as a shape in darkness that doesn't resolve into anything recognizable.  
    *Abilities:* Rank suppression (all System readouts near a Hollow One become unreliable). Reality distortion (local physics become inconsistent). Lattice consumption (Hollow Ones feed on Resonance — approaching one causes stat drain). At full expression, a single Hollow One can suppress an A-rank fighter to effective C-rank capability.  
    *Threat Level:* Varies by size. Small manifestations — D-rank threat. Mature Hollow One — S-rank threat. Ancient manifestation (encountered only once in recorded history, during the Third Dead System incident) — SSS-rank threat.  
    *Lore:* The Succession does not fear the Hollow Ones. They speak to them. The relationship between the Succession bloodlines and the Hollow Ones is the most classified information in the Sovereignty — more classified than the Dead Systems, more classified than the Void Gates. The Grand Marshal knows. The Sovereign knows. Three other people know. All three of those other people are dead.
    
    ---
    
    **Resonance Wolves (Lupus resonantis)**  
    *Classification: Class III*  
    *Native to: Multiple frontier worlds — believed to have been seeded intentionally by pre-System civilization*  
    *Appearance:* Canid predators, larger than any natural wolf — adult males 2m at shoulder. The fur shifts color with ambient Lattice density: white in low-resonance environments, blue in moderate, red near high-rank individuals. Eyes are solid amber with no pupil.  
    *Abilities:* Pack coordination through Lattice communication — they share tactical awareness across the pack in real time. Their bite disrupts Resonance flow — being bitten by a Resonance Wolf temporarily reduces the target's RESONANCE stat and can interrupt active abilities. Alpha specimens develop a gravity-manipulation ability at mature age.  
    *Threat Level:* Pack of 6+ — A-rank threat. Alpha alone — B-rank threat.  
    *Lore:* Resonance Wolves are one of the few creatures in the galaxy that willingly approach ranked fighters rather than fleeing them. They seem to be drawn to high Resonance signatures. The Aethon Dominion uses them as hunting companions and status symbols — a tamed Resonance Wolf indicates its owner is at least A-rank (below that, the wolf won't obey). The Dominion's S-rank bloodlines are said to run with entire packs.
    
    ---
    
    **Lattice Whales (Cetaceus latticus)**  
    *Classification: Class III*  
    *Native to: Deep space — the void between star systems*  
    *Appearance:* Organisms the size of small moons, composed partly of biological tissue and partly of crystallized Resonance that has accreted over millions of years. Appear as vast luminous shapes in deep space — blue-white, moving slowly. Visible to the naked eye from significant distance.  
    *Abilities:* Lattice manipulation at astronomical scale — a Lattice Whale in a system affects the local Resonance density for the entire system, usually elevating it. Their migration paths correspond to the highest-density Resonance regions of the galaxy. They do not attack unless provoked. When provoked, a single Lattice Whale can destroy a small fleet.  
    *Threat Level:* Do not provoke.  
    *Lore:* The Iron Covenant considers Lattice Whales sacred. Hunting one is a capital crime in Covenant territory and a Class-1 offense in Sovereignty law. The Aethon Dominion does not have a formal ban. There are rumors of Dominion researchers who have harvested crystallized Resonance from whale carcasses found in dead space. The ability amplifiers produced from this material are said to be unlike anything the standard augmentation industry produces.
    
    ---
    
    **Void Crawlers**  
    *Classification: Class IV — Sovereignty Denial Level: Classified*  
    *Known Locations: Void Gate aftermath zones*  
    *Appearance:* Small organisms — roughly the size of a human fist — that emerge from Void Gate activation sites. They appear as angular black geometric shapes that move in ways that suggest too many joints. They don't have eyes — they navigate by System signal. They hunt System chips.  
    *Abilities:* They can interface with rank chips directly — a Void Crawler that reaches a chip corrupts the readout. Multiple corruption types observed: false rank elevation (subject appears higher rank than reality), rank suppression (appears lower), and in three recorded cases, null corruption (chip reports subject as not present — as if they don't exist).  
    *Threat Level:* Individually, D-rank threat. In swarms (which is how they typically appear), B-rank threat. A corrupted chip is a permanent problem — standard removal requires Ranking Bureau intervention.  
    *Lore:* Ren Voss was exposed to Void Crawlers before his chip was installed. This is not a coincidence. Someone who understood what Void Crawlers do to chips brought them to the installation facility and released them at the moment of calibration. The corruption in Ren's chip is not a malfunction. It is a modification. Someone built the suppression into him using the Crawler's own mechanism, and they did it before he was old enough to remember.
    
    ---
    
    **Star Serpents (Serpentis solaris)**  
    *Classification: Class III*  
    *Native to: Stellar corona environments — live in and around active stars*  
    *Appearance:* Plasma-form organisms, 10–400km in length, that move through stellar corona plasma. From a ship at safe distance they appear as bright filaments in the stellar atmosphere. When they leave the star — which they do rarely — they trail plasma like comets.  
    *Abilities:* Extreme heat generation (obviously). Electromagnetic pulse generation that can disable ship systems. Lattice disruption at stellar scale — when a Star Serpent is agitated, the local Lattice becomes turbulent and rank advancement becomes dangerous. An agitated Star Serpent can interfere with FTL transit in its system.  
    *Threat Level:* Do not enter a system with an agitated Star Serpent unless you are at minimum S-rank and flying something you don't mind losing.  
    *Lore:* The Covenant believes Star Serpents are the galaxy's oldest living organisms — that they predate the current star population and have been living through stellar births and deaths for billions of years. Some Covenant philosophers argue that the Architect didn't create the rank system — it documented a system that organisms like the Star Serpents had been running instinctively for eons.
    
    ---
    
    **Dust Phantoms**  
    *Classification: Class II*  
    *Native to: Gas giants, nebulae, high-particle-density space environments*  
    *Appearance:* Semi-transparent organism composed of ionized gas and particulate matter. Shape-shifting — typically appear as vaguely humanoid forms 3–8m tall when in low-particle environments, expanding to kilometer-scale formations in dense nebulae.  
    *Abilities:* Can absorb and re-emit Resonance — essentially acting as living Lattice relay nodes. Ranked individuals can channel abilities through a Dust Phantom at dramatically extended range. This property is exploited by S-rank fighters to extend their area of effect across planetary distances.  
    *Threat Level:* Non-aggressive to anything below SS-rank. To SS-rank and above, treat as territorial.  
    *Lore:* Nobody knows if Dust Phantoms are intelligent. They respond to ability use in ways that suggest awareness. They have been observed gathering around Void Gate activation sites — not fleeing, not attacking, just watching.
