    # PART THIRTEEN: LANGUAGES, COMMUNICATION & INFORMATION SYSTEMS
    
    ## The Linguistic Landscape of the Sovereignty
    
    The Sovereignty administers communication across approximately 4,200 star systems containing over 40 trillion beings. The solution to linguistic diversity was not organic — it was imposed. Over 3,800 years, the Iron Covenant's educational apparatus systematically elevated one language to universal status while allowing regional tongues to survive as cultural artifacts rather than functional necessities.
    
    ---
    
    ## THE STANDARD TONGUE (Aethonic Common)
    
    **Origin:** A synthetic language constructed 3,800 years ago by Covenant linguists who analyzed 140 existing languages and extracted the most efficient phoneme sets, grammatical structures, and information-density principles from each.  
    **Speakers:** ~38 trillion (essentially all Sovereignty citizens)  
    **Writing System:** The Imperial Script — geometric, angular characters designed for maximum legibility at distance and maximum compression in data systems. 94 base characters. No ambiguous phoneme mappings.
    
    **Why It Works:**  
    Standard Aethonic was designed for a galaxy — not a nation. Every phoneme is pronounceable by every known sapient species (specific accommodations were made for Velhari sub-vocal range and Keth'Sari tonal register). The grammar was designed so that rank status can be indicated by grammatical particle rather than word choice — meaning a speaker's rank is always structurally clear without requiring explicit statement.
    
    **The Class Gradient in Language:**  
    - **High Aethonic:** Formal register used in governance, legal proceedings, and Covenant scholarship. More complex grammatical structures, larger vocabulary, more nuanced rank-particle system. Speaking High Aethonic fluently signals education and rank access.
    - **Common Aethonic:** The everyday standard. Clear, functional, slightly flattened rank-particle use.
    - **Low Aethonic:** Frontier and Undercity variant. Grammatical compression, rank-particle omission (a deliberate cultural statement in Unranked communities), heavy loan-word integration from regional tongues.
    - **The Undercity Cant:** Not a dialect — a separate coded language used in Undercities and black market contexts. Surface-level it sounds like degraded Low Aethonic. Underneath, it carries encoded meaning through specific word substitutions and tonal patterns that rank-chip translation programs don't capture. Ren Voss is fluent in Cant. Most Sovereignty officials are not.
    
    ---
    
    ## REGIONAL & SPECIES LANGUAGES
    
    **Vel-Tongue (Velhari Native Language)**  
    A tonal language with 12 tonal registers that carry grammatical meaning. The Velhari speak Standard Aethonic in most contexts, but among themselves in private they use Vel-Tongue as a cultural preservation practice. The Sovereignty permitted this — the Covenant argued that the Velhari's oral tradition contained ecological and pharmaceutical knowledge worth preserving. The deeper reason: the Covenant's scholars believe specific Vel-Tongue tonal patterns interact directly with the Lattice, and they have been quietly studying this for 300 years.
    
    **Keth'Sari Philosophical Dialect**  
    The inhabitants of Keth'Sara developed a variant of Standard Aethonic that integrates philosophical precision into everyday speech. Every noun carries a metaphysical qualifier. Every verb carries a certainty indicator. "The door is open" in Keth'Sari Dialect translates literally as "The door currently presents itself as open to my perception with high confidence." The Covenant adores this. Everyone else finds it exhausting.
    
    **Succession Glyphic**  
    Not a spoken language — a written system used exclusively by Succession members for secure communication. 340 glyphs that represent concepts rather than phonemes. The glyphs interact with each other — adjacent glyphs modify each other's meaning in ways that make the system impossible to translate without understanding the full context of surrounding glyphs. The Void Research Directorate has captured hundreds of documents in Succession Glyphic. They have partially decoded 12. The other documents remain unread. The twelve decoded ones contained nothing operationally useful — which either means the Succession's secure communications are genuinely unbreakable, or those twelve documents were bait.
    
    ---
    
    ## COMMUNICATION TECHNOLOGY
    
    ### The System Network (The Web)
    Every rank chip is a node in a galaxy-spanning communication network administered by the Ranking Bureau. The Web enables:
    - Instant chip-to-chip communication within a star system
    - Delayed (light-speed-limited) communication across deep space (compensated by relay stations)
    - Emergency broadcasts that can override all chip communication simultaneously
    - Real-time rank tracking of all registered individuals
    - The ability for the Bureau to read, filter, or suppress chip communication without the user's knowledge or consent
    
    **The Web's dark capability:** The Bureau can push information into a chip — not just receive it. This has been used twice in documented history for emergency system-wide announcements. It has been used approximately 400 times for undocumented purposes according to Covenant archive data that the Bureau has never confirmed or denied.
    
    ### Darkline Communication
    A parallel communication system operating outside the Web. Used by: the Compliance Division, the Void Research Directorate, Free Captains, smugglers, the Succession, and anyone with enough technical knowledge to build a Darkline transmitter. Darkline operates on Resonance-frequency bands the Web's filters are not calibrated to detect. Message transmission is slower and range-limited but invisible to Bureau monitoring.
    
    ### The Succession's Deep-Channel
    A theoretical communication method documented only in intelligence reports: Succession members above VI-40 appear to be able to communicate through the Endless Dark directly — not through any physical medium. The Deep-Channel, if it exists, has zero latency regardless of distance, cannot be intercepted by any known system, and leaves no record in any detectable medium. The Void Research Directorate's assessment: the Deep-Channel exists. They cannot prove it. They cannot tap it. This keeps the Directorate's senior staff awake at night.

    # PART FOURTEEN: ECONOMY, CURRENCY & RESOURCE SYSTEMS
    
    ## The Economic Architecture of the Sovereignty
    
    The Sovereignty's economy is a tiered market system in which legal access to goods, services, and economic participation is explicitly calibrated to rank. An Unranked individual and an S-rank individual live in economies that overlap but are not the same — different currencies, different markets, different legal frameworks.
    
    ---
    
    ## CURRENCY SYSTEMS
    
    ### Sovereign Credits (SC)
    The official universal currency of the Sovereignty. Digital — stored on rank chip, transferred through the Web. Backed by the Sovereignty's Resonance crystal reserve — every SC is theoretically redeemable for a precise weight of processed crystal. In practice, nobody has ever tried to redeem a significant amount. The reserve's actual size is classified.
    
    **Rank-Gated Transactions:**
    - SC transactions above certain thresholds require minimum rank to authorize
    - Unranked individuals can hold SC but cannot authorize transactions above 500 SC without a ranked co-signer
    - D-rank: 10,000 SC limit per transaction
    - C-rank: 100,000 SC
    - B-rank: 1,000,000 SC
    - A-rank: Unlimited with Bureau logging
    - S-rank: Unlimited without logging (this is the threshold at which financial privacy begins)
    
    ### Black Coin (BC)
    The Undercity and frontier's non-chip currency. Physical tokens — small crystallized Resonance fragments of standardized weight and purity. Untraceable. Not Web-connected. A Black Coin transaction exists only in the physical world and in the memories of the parties involved. The Sovereignty periodically attempts to suppress Black Coin circulation. The economy of the Unranked depends on it. The attempts are unsuccessful.
    
    **Conversion:** 1 Black Coin ≈ 40 SC at standard exchange. In the frontier, 1 Black Coin ≈ 60–80 SC due to the premium on untraceable transaction.
    
    ### Guild Marks (GM)
    The Merchant Guild's proprietary currency. Accepted across all Guild-affiliated trading posts (approximately 80% of commercial space in the Sovereignty). Guild Marks carry no rank-gate — any being can hold and transfer GM regardless of rank status. This is the primary reason the Guilds are politically tolerated despite operating a parallel financial system: they are the only functional economic bridge between the ranked and Unranked populations.
    
    ---
    
    ## RESOURCE ECONOMICS
    
    ### Resonance Crystal — The Strategic Resource
    Everything in the Sovereignty's economy ultimately traces to Resonance crystal. It powers ships (Fold Drives), powers weapons (Resonance cannons), forms the physical medium of rank chips, backs the currency, and is the primary material for ability amplification equipment. Whoever controls Resonance crystal mining controls the galaxy.
    
    **The Sovereignty's monopoly:** By law, all Resonance crystal mining operations must be licensed by the Bureau of Resources — a Sovereignty body. 89% of active mines are Sovereignty-operated or Sovereignty-contracted. The remaining 11% are Dominion-operated under historical treaty provisions. Free Captain and independent operations exist but are technically illegal. Technically.
    
    **The Lattice Whale Problem:** The richest Resonance crystal deposits are not geological — they are biological. Lattice Whale carcasses contain crystallized Resonance of 400% higher purity than the best geological deposit. The Sovereignty made hunting them illegal 600 years ago. The black market for Whale-grade crystal exists, is enormous, and is largely ignored as long as it doesn't become visible. The Dominion's augmentation houses run exclusively on Whale-grade. Nobody asks where they get it.
    
    ### The Pharmaceutical Economy
    The second-most economically significant sector. Dominated by the Covenant's medical division and three large Sovereignty-licensed pharmaceutical houses. The core inputs — Seraphwillow extract, Resonance Moss compounds, Velhari botanical derivatives — are all regulated as Covenant-controlled resources. The result: the Covenant's medical division is the most profitable single organization in the galaxy. Their operating surplus funds the Archive system, the Academy network, and several operations that don't appear in published budgets.
    
    ### The Augmentation Economy
    The third-most significant sector. More fragmented — dozens of legal and semi-legal operators, with the Dominion's houses at the top end and frontier chop-shops at the bottom. The legal augmentation economy is approximately 12 trillion SC per year. The illegal augmentation economy — covering Transgression-class modifications, Void augmentations, and gray-market military-grade work — is estimated by Guild intelligence at 4 trillion SC per year. The Sovereignty's enforcement spend on the illegal side: 200 billion SC per year. The math says the enforcement budget is insufficient. The Sovereignty knows this. The augmentation lobby (of which the Dominion's houses are the largest members) has been ensuring the enforcement budget stays insufficient for 300 years.

    # PART FIFTEEN: TTRPG MECHANICS — THE COMPLETE GAME SYSTEM
    
    ## Core Design Philosophy
    VOID ASCENDANT: THE ETERNAL HIERARCHY as a TTRPG uses the source novel as both narrative foundation and mechanical inspiration. The game is designed to capture the manhwa power-progression feeling — the visceral satisfaction of advancement, the weight of rank disparity, the moment when a "weak" character demonstrates capability that the powerful cannot explain. Mechanically, it draws from:
    - **The stat system** from the Series Bible (FORCE, SPEED, ENDURANCE, RESONANCE, PERCEPTION, COGNITION)
    - **The rank tier structure** (F through SSS)
    - **Ability development** as organic character growth
    - **The political layer** as a consequence system that responds to character action
    
    ---
    
    ## THE DICE SYSTEM
    
    **Core Mechanic: The Resonance Roll**  
    All checks use a pool of d10s equal to the relevant stat value. Count successes (7+ on any die = 1 success). Threshold varies by difficulty:
    
    | Difficulty | Success Threshold | Description |
    |------------|------------------|-------------|
    | Trivial | 1 success | Any ranked individual can do this |
    | Simple | 2 successes | Standard competent action |
    | Moderate | 3 successes | Requires focus and skill |
    | Hard | 5 successes | Pushing capability |
    | Extreme | 7 successes | Near the edge of what's possible at rank |
    | Impossible | 10+ successes | Beyond rated rank capability |
    | Void | Special | Requires Void Index expenditure |
    
    **Rank Bonus Dice:**  
    Every rank tier above E adds bonus dice to all checks:
    - E-rank: Base stat dice only
    - D-rank: +1d10 to all checks
    - C-rank: +2d10
    - B-rank: +4d10
    - A-rank: +7d10
    - S-rank: +11d10
    - SS-rank: +16d10
    - SSS-rank: +22d10
    
    This creates the power curve the manhwa genre demands — a D-rank fighter attempting something an A-rank does casually is not merely at a disadvantage, they are operating in a fundamentally different resolution space.

    ## CHARACTER CREATION
    
    ### Step 1: Choose Species
    Each species provides a stat array and a starting ability pool.
    
    **Human (Homo Stellaris)**  
    Base Stats: FORCE 3, SPEED 3, ENDURANCE 3, RESONANCE 3, PERCEPTION 3, COGNITION 3  
    Species Ability: *Adaptability* — once per session, reroll any stat check and take the better result  
    Starting Rank: D (with chip installed) or F (Unranked)
    
    **Velhari**  
    Base Stats: FORCE 4, SPEED 2, ENDURANCE 5, RESONANCE 3, PERCEPTION 2, COGNITION 2  
    Species Ability: *Dense Biology* — reduce all incoming physical damage by 1 success worth  
    Starting Rank: D or F
    
    **Keth'Sari**  
    Base Stats: FORCE 1, SPEED 3, ENDURANCE 2, RESONANCE 6, PERCEPTION 4, COGNITION 4  
    Species Ability: *Lattice Sensitivity* — can perceive System readouts without a scanner; double bonus dice for RESONANCE checks  
    Starting Rank: D (Keth'Sari have near-universal chip installation)
    
    **Veth-Born (Ghost World Human Variant)**  
    Base Stats: FORCE 2, SPEED 4, ENDURANCE 2, RESONANCE 3, PERCEPTION 5, COGNITION 2  
    Species Ability: *Dark-Touched* — start with Void Index 5 (benefits and complications); immune to first Void exposure event per session  
    Starting Rank: F or D (Veth ranking is irregular)
    
    **Succession Bloodline (Rare — GM Approval)**  
    Base Stats: FORCE 3, SPEED 3, ENDURANCE 3, RESONANCE 4, PERCEPTION 3, COGNITION 2  
    Starting Void Index: 15  
    Species Ability: *The Memory* — once per session, draw on the Endless Dark. Make one impossible check as if it were Extreme difficulty. Gain 1d6 Void Index.  
    Starting Rank: F (System cannot read them correctly) or assigned by GM
    
    ---
    
    ### Step 2: Assign Starting Stats
    Distribute 6 additional points across the six primary stats. No single stat may exceed 8 at character creation. Secondary stats (AUTHORITY, STEALTH, ADAPTABILITY, VOID INDEX) begin at base values per species.
    
    ### Step 3: Choose Starting Rank
    Most campaigns begin at D or C rank. Starting rank determines:
    - Available ability tier (D-rank: Tier 1 abilities only)
    - Legal standing (F/E rank: no legal personhood in Sovereignty territory)
    - Starting resource access
    - Social starting position
    
    ### Step 4: Select Starting Abilities (D-rank characters)
    Choose 2 Tier 1 abilities from the Ability Catalog (see Part 15b). These represent the first manifestations of Resonance the character has achieved. Abilities should reflect the character's stat distribution and personal history.
    
    ---
    
    ## THE RANK ADVANCEMENT SYSTEM
    
    Advancement in VOID ASCENDANT is not level-based — it is narrative-triggered. Characters advance rank when they have demonstrated capability at the threshold of their current rank through play.
    
    **Advancement Triggers:**
    - Complete a narrative arc requiring performance at the upper limit of current rank
    - Defeat or overcome a challenge rated 1–2 rank tiers above current rank
    - Unlock an ability that pushes beyond current tier (triggers advancement to enable it)
    - GM determination based on story significance
    
    **The Advancement Event:**  
    When triggered, the player declares their character is in Advancement State. The GM narrates a moment of rank transition — physical effects, System notification, stat changes. The player then:
    1. Gains rank bonus dice
    2. Selects a new Tier ability (one tier higher than previous maximum)
    3. All stats gain points equal to new rank tier (D-rank: +2 each; C-rank: +3 each; B-rank: +5 each; A-rank: +8 each)
    4. Rolls Advancement Shock check (ENDURANCE vs difficulty = new rank tier × 2). Failure = Advancement Shock complications

    ## ABILITY CATALOG — SAMPLE ABILITIES
    
    ### TIER 1 ABILITIES (D-Rank)
    **Iron Skin** *(Passive — ENDURANCE primary)*: Reduce all incoming physical damage successes by 2. Does not stack with armor.
    
    **Surge Step** *(Active — SPEED primary)*: Move up to 3× normal distance in a single action. Terrain is not an obstacle. Duration: 1 action.
    
    **System Sight** *(Passive — PERCEPTION primary)*: See rank readouts without a scanner. Range equal to PERCEPTION score in meters.
    
    **Echo Fist** *(Active — FORCE primary)*: Melee strike sends a Resonance shockwave to the same target 1 second after impact. Second hit cannot be blocked. Does FORCE stat value in automatic successes.
    
    **Lattice Read** *(Passive — RESONANCE primary)*: Passively sense ability use within RESONANCE score in meters. Cannot be surprised by ability activation within range.
    
    ---
    
    ### TIER 2 ABILITIES (C-Rank)
    **Gravity Anchor** *(Active — FORCE/RESONANCE)*: Fix a target in place. Target cannot move for duration. Duration: RESONANCE score in rounds. Resisted by FORCE+ENDURANCE.
    
    **Phantom Step** *(Active — SPEED/STEALTH)*: Move without producing System signature for 1 round per activation. Rank chips cannot track you. Detection requires PERCEPTION check at Extreme difficulty.
    
    **Resonance Armor** *(Active — ENDURANCE/RESONANCE)*: Create a Lattice-energy barrier. Absorbs incoming damage equal to (RESONANCE × 3) successes before failing. Requires 1 action to activate. Duration: until destroyed or dismissed.
    
    ---
    
    ### TIER 3 ABILITIES (B-Rank)
    **Gravity Pulse** *(Active — FORCE/RESONANCE)*: Release omnidirectional gravitational burst. All targets within FORCE score meters are pushed back and suffer kinetic damage. Terrain within range is visibly distorted for 1 round.
    
    **Compound Strike Sequence** *(Active — SPEED/FORCE)*: Execute up to SPEED score attacks in a single action, each targeting different locations on the same target or different targets within range. Each attack at standard resolution.
    
    **Perception Lock** *(Active — PERCEPTION)*: Choose a target. For PERCEPTION score rounds, you cannot lose track of that target regardless of obstacles, stealth abilities, or System suppression.
    
    ---
    
    ### TIER 4 ABILITIES (A-Rank)
    **Pressure Field** *(Active — FORCE/RESONANCE)*: Create a zone of elevated gravitational force centered on self, radius RESONANCE score meters. All beings within the field suffer −4d10 to all physical checks. Ability use within the field requires a RESONANCE check or fails.
    
    **Dead Zone** *(Active — RESONANCE)*: Activate a field within RESONANCE score meters in which no abilities function except yours. Duration: COGNITION score rounds. Activation is visible — bright white pulse at initiation.
    
    ---
    
    ### VOID ABILITIES (Succession Characters Only)
    **Void Step** *(Active — VOID INDEX)*: Spend 3 VI. Step out of physical space for 1 round — become undetectable, untargetable, and unable to act. Return to physical space anywhere within RESONANCE score meters.
    
    **The Memory** *(Active — VOID INDEX)*: Spend 5 VI. For 1 round, your attacks bypass all Lattice-based defenses. Resonance Armor, Iron Skin, and similar abilities provide no protection against your actions.
    
    **Hollow Touch** *(Active — VOID INDEX)*: Spend 2 VI. On successful melee touch, inflict VI equal to your RESONANCE score on the target. If target reaches VI 20+, begin rolling for Void Exposure complications.

    ## THE VOID INDEX COMPLICATION SYSTEM
    
    Characters with Void Index accumulate effects as VI increases:
    
    | VI Range | Effect |
    |----------|--------|
    | 0–10 | No mechanical effect. Slight visual tell (slightly darker ambiance). |
    | 11–20 | +2d10 to all RESONANCE checks. −1d10 to all COGNITION checks (the Dark is louder than thought). |
    | 21–35 | +4d10 RESONANCE. −2d10 COGNITION. System readout begins showing anomalies. Void Abilities become available if not already. |
    | 36–50 | +6d10 RESONANCE. −3d10 COGNITION and AUTHORITY. The character begins attracting attention from Void-sensitive entities. |
    | 51–70 | Full Void Sensitivity — can perceive the Endless Dark directly. +10d10 RESONANCE. −5d10 COGNITION. Character begins receiving "memories that are not theirs." GM introduces Endless Dark-sourced information. |
    | 71–90 | The character is more Endless Dark than material being. All Tier 1–4 abilities cost no resources. Void Abilities are free. Standard medicine no longer works. Only Hollow Surgery can address physical damage. |
    | 91–99 | The Return approaches. Character is visibly wrong — light bends around them. The System cannot read them. Succession leadership attempts contact. |
    | 100 | The Return. Character dissolves into the Endless Dark. This is the character's story ending — but not death. What they become is up to the table. |

    ## THE POLITICAL CONSEQUENCE SYSTEM
    
    Unlike most TTRPG systems, VOID ASCENDANT tracks political heat as a mechanical resource. Every significant action the party takes registers with the four major factions on a track from 0 (unnoticed) to 10 (active intervention).
    
    **Faction Heat Tracks:**
    - Sovereignty Heat (SH)
    - Covenant Heat (CH)
    - Dominion Heat (DH)
    - Succession Heat (SCH)
    
    **Escalation Events by Heat Level:**
    - 1–3: Passive monitoring. System flags on party members. Rumor mill activity.
    - 4–5: Active surveillance. Plainclothes Compliance Division. Information gathering.
    - 6–7: Formal investigation. Named agent assigned. Known associates monitored.
    - 8–9: Direct intervention. Combat-capable assets deployed. Bounties possible.
    - 10: Total response. The faction commits full resources. This is the endgame arc trigger.
    
    **Managing Heat:** Every session the party takes no action flagged by a faction, reduce that faction's heat by 1 (minimum 0). Bribes, information trades, and alliance actions can reduce heat faster. The Covenant reduces heat differently — Covenant Heat decreases when the party demonstrates preservation of knowledge, assistance to Covenant operations, or adherence to rank law.

    ## SAMPLE ADVENTURE SEEDS
    
    **The Suppressed File**  
    A Ranking Bureau data technician contacts the party — she found a chip suppression record buried in the Bureau's archive from 20 years ago. The record shows the suppression protocol and the authorization chain. The name at the top of the authorization: a member of the Grand Council. The technician is already being followed. She has three hours before the Compliance Division arrives at her apartment.
    
    **The Whale Road**  
    A Free Captain hires the party to escort a smuggling run. The cargo: Whale-grade Resonance crystal, enough to fund a small army. The buyer: unknown, delivered to a dead drop in the frontier. Three other parties want the crystal — the Dominion, a Guild house, and someone who arrived in a Wraith-Class ship and has been following the cargo since it left the harvesting site.
    
    **Veth Below**  
    A Covenant researcher disappeared on Veth three weeks ago. He was studying the subsurface ghost-lights. His last transmission mentioned finding a chamber that "the Succession built before the System existed." The party is hired to retrieve him — or what remains of him. The Veth locals are polite, unhelpful, and watching very carefully to see what the party does when they find the chamber.
    
    **The Advancement**  
    A local fighter is about to advance to S-rank — an event that will be the first S-rank in this region in a generation. Three parties want to control the moment: the Sovereignty (wanting a loyal S-rank asset), the Succession (wanting to recruit), and someone unknown who has been in the city for two weeks asking questions about the fighter's bloodline history. The fighter just wants to advance without dying. The party is their security.
